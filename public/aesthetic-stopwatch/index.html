Why use an aesthetic stopwatch?

A stopwatch is one of the simplest productivity tools — start it, work, stop it. But most stopwatches feel like tools. An aesthetic stopwatch makes timing your work feel intentional rather than mechanical. StudyFlow wraps the stopwatch in a complete focus environment that rewards every minute you put in.

  • Track any session: Start the stopwatch whenever you begin studying — no preset durations needed
  • Beautiful display: Clean, large typography against your choice of 50+ living wallpapers
  • Ambient soundscapes: Rain, fireplace, lo-fi, and white noise play alongside your stopwatch
  • Earn XP for every second: Every stopwatch session counts toward your XP, streaks, and levels

Stopwatch features

  • Start, stop, and reset: Simple one-click controls for timing your study sessions
  • Session history: Every stopwatch session is saved with date, duration, and optional task link
