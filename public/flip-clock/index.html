What is a flip clock?

A flip clock is a type of clock that uses a split-flap display — each digit is printed on a set of physical flaps that flip down to reveal the next number. Unlike a standard digital display where digits stay static until they change, a flip clock creates a small mechanical event every time a digit advances. This subtle motion is what gives flip clocks their distinctive character.

Originally used in airport departure boards and railway station displays, the split-flap mechanism was prized for its reliability and readability. Today, online flip clocks recreate the same visual effect digitally, often with smoother animations, customisable colours, and integration with productivity features like Pomodoro timers.
