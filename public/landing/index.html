Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about StudyFlow.

Is StudyFlow really free?

Yes. StudyFlow is completely free with no sign-up required. All features including the Pomodoro timer, ambient sounds, wallpapers, task management, and gamification are available without payment.

What makes StudyFlow different from other focus timers?

StudyFlow combines a Pomodoro timer with living wallpapers, ambient soundscapes, task management, and gamification into one seamless experience. It is designed specifically for students, not corporate productivity. The visual environment adapts to your mood, and the gamification system keeps you coming back.

Does StudyFlow work on mobile?

Yes. StudyFlow is fully responsive and works on any device with a browser. You can also install it as a Progressive Web App (PWA) for a native-like experience on your phone or tablet.

What is Nova?

Nova is your StudyFlow companion — a virtual productivity pet that responds to your focus sessions. The more you study, the more Nova grows. It adds a layer of warmth and accountability to your study routine.

Can I use StudyFlow with my friends?

Yes. StudyFlow includes a leaderboard where you can see how your focus time compares with other students. You can compete for the top spot and motivate each other to stay consistent.
