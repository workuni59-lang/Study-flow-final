FAQ

Frequently asked
questions.

What is a Pomodoro timer?+
A Pomodoro timer implements the Pomodoro Technique: 25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break. After four cycles, take a longer 15-30 minute break. It is one of the most effective methods for maintaining concentration and preventing mental fatigue.
Is StudyFlow really free?+
Yes. The Pomodoro timer, all wallpapers, all soundscapes, session tracking, and gamification are completely free. No account, no credit card, no time limits.
Can I use it without signing up?+
Absolutely. Open StudyFlow and start a timer immediately. A free account is optional — only needed if you want your history synced across devices.
Can I change the durations?+
Yes. StudyFlow offers classic Pomodoro (25/5), deep focus (50/10), Flow mode, and fully custom durations for both work and break intervals.
Does it work on mobile?+
Yes. Works in any browser on any device. You can also install it as a PWA for a native app experience on your phone or tablet.
What makes StudyFlow different?+
Unlike basic timers, StudyFlow wraps the Pomodoro technique in a complete focus environment with living wallpapers, ambient sounds, gamification, and session analytics.
