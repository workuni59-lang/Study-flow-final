Why use a study planner?

A study planner helps you break down your curriculum into manageable subjects and topics. Instead of wondering what to study, you open your planner and see exactly what needs attention. StudyFlow takes this further by integrating your planner with a focus timer and progress analytics.

  • Subject organization: Create subjects, add topics, and track mastery levels
  • Mastery tracking: Mark topics as Red (starting), Amber (reviewing), or Green (mastered)
  • Spotlight system: The dashboard highlights your weakest subject so you know where to focus
  • Integrated timer: Start a focus session linked to any subject or task

Study Planner features

  • Create subjects: Add courses like Mathematics, Biology, or History with custom colors
  • Add topics: Break each subject into specific topics (e.g., "Organic Chemistry" under Chemistry)
  • Three mastery levels: Track progress with Red/Amber/Green for each topic
  • Auto-advancement: Completing a task automatically advances the linked topic's mastery level
  • Detailed analytics: See subject-wise progress, topic breakdowns, and focus time per subject
  • Task integration: Create tasks linked to subjects and topics for focused study sessions

Frequently asked questions

Can I create multiple subjects?

Yes. There is no limit to the number of subjects you can create. Each subject can have its own color and list of topics.

How does mastery tracking work?

Each topic has three levels: Red (just starting), Amber (reviewing), and Green (mastered). You can update mastery manually or let it advance automatically when you complete linked tasks.

Can I link tasks to specific subjects?

Yes. When creating a task, you can select a subject and topic. Completed tasks automatically advance the topic's mastery level from Red to Amber to Green.

Does the planner sync across devices?

Yes. Create a free account and your subjects, topics, tasks, and progress sync automatically across all your devices.
