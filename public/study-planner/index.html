FAQ
Frequently asked
questions.

Can I create multiple subjects with different colors?+
Yes. There is no limit on subjects. Each one gets a custom color for quick visual identification in your dashboard.
How does mastery tracking work?+
Each topic has three levels: Red (just starting), Amber (reviewing), and Green (mastered). Levels can be set manually or advance automatically when you complete linked study tasks.
Can I link tasks to specific subjects?+
Yes. Every task can be assigned to a subject and topic. Completing it advances the topic's mastery level from Red to Amber to Green automatically.
Do I need an account to use the planner?+
No. The planner works immediately without sign-up. Create an account only if you want your data to sync across devices.
What is the spotlight feature?+
The dashboard automatically highlights your subject with the lowest mastery percentage. It removes the question of "what should I study today?" — the answer is right there.
