Why use a dedicated study timer?

A study timer does more than count minutes — it structures your focus. By alternating between deep work sessions and breaks, you maintain mental freshness for hours. StudyFlow enhances this with an immersive environment that makes you want to start each session.

  • Pomodoro mode: 25-minute sprints for everyday studying
  • Deep focus mode: 50-minute sessions for demanding material
  • Flow mode: Adapts session length based on your concentration
  • Visual environment: 50+ wallpapers that transform your browser into a study space

Frequently asked questions

How is a study timer different from a regular timer?

A study timer is designed for the Pomodoro Technique and similar focus methods. It automatically alternates between work and break periods, tracks your history, and provides an environment optimized for concentration.

Can I use StudyFlow without creating an account?

Yes. The timer and all features work immediately without sign-up. Create an account only if you want your data to sync across devices.

Does it work on phones and tablets?

Yes. StudyFlow is fully responsive and works on any device. You can install it as a PWA for a native-like experience on mobile.

Can I track multiple subjects?

Yes. StudyFlow has a built-in subject and topic system. Create subjects (like Biology or Calculus), add topics, and track your mastery level for each one.
