FAQ
Frequently asked
questions.

How is a study timer different from a regular timer?+
A study timer is built for the Pomodoro Technique. It automatically alternates between work and break periods, tracks your history, and provides an environment optimized for concentration — not just counting seconds.
Can I use StudyFlow without signing up?+
Yes. The timer and all features work immediately with no account. Create an account only if you want your data to sync across devices.
Does it work on phones and tablets?+
Yes. StudyFlow works on any device with a browser. Install it as a PWA for a native app experience on mobile.
Can I track multiple subjects?+
Yes. Create subjects like Biology or Calculus, add topics, and track mastery levels (Red/Amber/Green) for each one. The dashboard highlights your weakest subject so you know where to focus.
What focus modes are available?+
StudyFlow offers classic Pomodoro (25 minutes work, 5 minutes break), deep focus (50 minutes work, 10 minutes break), and Flow mode that adapts session length to your concentration level in real time.
