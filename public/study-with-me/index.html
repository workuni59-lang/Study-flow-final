FAQ
Still curious?
Let us answer that.

What exactly is a study-with-me timer?+
Think of it as your virtual study buddy. Instead of watching someone else study on YouTube, you get your own cozy corner with a Pomodoro timer, aesthetic wallpapers that shift and breathe, ambient sounds that wrap around you, and gentle progress tracking that celebrates every session. It is the feeling of studying beside someone — without the distraction.
Do I need to create an account?+
Not at all. Open the app, pick your wallpaper and sound, and start your first session immediately. An account is optional and only useful if you want your XP, streaks, and history to follow you across devices.
Can I use this on my phone during a commute?+
Absolutely. StudyFlow works in any browser — on your laptop, tablet, or phone. You can install it as a PWA and it behaves just like a native app, complete with your wallpapers and sounds, no App Store needed.
Can I personalize my study space?+
Yes — that is the whole idea. Choose from over fifty wallpapers (pastel gradients, forest scenes, cozy desk corners, cosmic skies), pick a soundscape that matches your mood (lo-fi, rain, fireplace, white noise, coffee shop), and switch between Pomodoro, deep focus, or Flow mode depending on how much time you have. Your space, your rules.
How is this different from a normal Pomodoro app?+
A normal timer just counts minutes. StudyFlow builds a room around you. The wallpapers are animated, the sounds are layered, the gamification is designed to encourage consistency (not perfection), and every element — from the fonts to the colors — exists to make studying feel less like a chore and more like a ritual you look forward to.
What focus modes are available?+
Classic Pomodoro (25 minutes of focus, 5 minutes to breathe), deep focus (50 minutes for those flow-state sessions, followed by a 10-minute reset), and Flow mode that adapts to your concentration level in real time. Each mode works with every wallpaper and sound, so your perfect combination is always one click away.
