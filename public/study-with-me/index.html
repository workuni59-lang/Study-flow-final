What is a study with me timer?+
It recreates the cozy, focused atmosphere of popular study-with-me YouTube videos. StudyFlow adds aesthetic wallpapers, ambient soundscapes, and Pomodoro timing so every session feels like studying alongside a friend.
Can I use this without signing up?+
Yes. Everything works immediately with no account. Create an account only if you want your progress and stats to sync across devices.
Does it work on my phone?+
Yes. StudyFlow works on any device with a browser. You can install it as a PWA for a native app experience on your phone or tablet.
Can I study with friends?+
While live co-working is coming soon, you can already share session stats, compare streaks, and motivate each other through leaderboards and achievements.
What focus modes are there?+
Classic Pomodoro (25 minutes work, 5 break), deep focus (50 minutes work, 10 break), and Flow mode that adapts to your concentration level. Perfect for study-with-me sessions of any length.
