Features you will not find anywhere else

  • Living wallpapers. Your background is not a static image. It shifts, breathes, and sets the mood before your timer even starts. Animated gradients, serene photos, and deep color atmospheres that make the app feel like a place, not a tool.
  • Zen mode. A distraction-free environment where the timer, the wallpaper, and the ambient sound exist together. No sidebar, no tabs, no notifications. Just you and your work.
  • Adaptive focus modes. Classic Pomodoro at 25 minutes, deep focus at 50, or Flow mode that extends your session when you are in the zone and eases up when you are not.
  • Ambient soundscapes. Rain, fireplace, lo-fi beats, white noise — sounds designed to fade into the background so your thoughts can stay in the foreground.
  • Gamification that makes sense. Earn XP for every completed session. Level up, unlock badges, and build streaks. The game is consistency, and the reward is seeing your progress grow.
  • Built-in task management. Attach a task to every focus session. Check it off when you are done. Small wins stack up faster than you think.
