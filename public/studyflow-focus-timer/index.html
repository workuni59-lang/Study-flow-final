FAQ
Still have questions?
We have answers.

What is the StudyFlow focus timer?+
StudyFlow is a free, browser-based focus timer with animated wallpapers, ambient sounds, and gamified progress tracking. It is designed for students who want their study space to feel like a place, not a tool. Unlike a simple countdown, StudyFlow builds a room around you with living backgrounds, layered soundscapes, and gentle encouragement that makes you want to come back.
How is this different from other focus timers?+
Most timers just count minutes. StudyFlow combines a Pomodoro timer with immersive living wallpapers that shift and breathe, ambient soundscapes that wrap around you, and gamification that celebrates consistency — not perfection. It is built specifically for students who need a distraction-free environment that actually feels good to sit in.
Do I need to create an account?+
Not at all. Open the app, pick your wallpaper and sound, and start your first session immediately. An account is completely optional and only useful if you want your XP, streaks, and study history to sync across devices.
What focus modes are available?+
Three modes: classic Pomodoro (25 minutes focus, 5 minutes break) for structured sprints, deep focus (50 minutes with a 10-minute reset) for flow-state work, and Flow mode that adapts to your concentration level in real time. Every mode works with every wallpaper and sound.
Can I personalize the look and feel?+
Yes — that is the whole idea. Choose from over fifty wallpapers across categories like forests, urban nights, cozy interiors, cosmic skies, and abstract gradients. Layer on a soundscape that matches your mood — lo-fi, rain, fireplace, white noise, coffee shop. Adjust blur, brightness, and saturation. Your space, your rules.
Is StudyFlow really free?+
Yes. StudyFlow is completely free with no sign-up required. All features — every wallpaper, every sound, every focus mode, and all gamification — are available without payment. There is no limited trial, no hidden paywall, and no credit card asked.
